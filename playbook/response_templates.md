# Reply templates for price-match requests

Short, polite, factual. Always: thank, say what was checked, give the decision, give the reason, offer a next step.
Amounts in square brackets come from the Excel `PriceMatch_Calc` sheet (the "Reply to the partner" cell fills them in).

## 1. Accepted - discount

> Thank you for flagging this. We checked the offer: same room, dates and conditions. We have adjusted our price by
> EUR [discount] to EUR [new price]. The new rate is valid for 24 hours. Thank you for booking with us.

## 2. Accepted - better supplier found

> Thank you for flagging this. We found the same room through another of our suppliers and can now offer
> EUR [price] with the same conditions (meal plan and cancellation policy unchanged). The rate is valid for 24 hours.

## 3. Refused - not the same product (non-refundable, no breakfast, taxes)

> Thank you for flagging this. We checked the offer carefully: it is not the same product. The competitor rate is
> non-refundable and excludes taxes (about EUR [taxes]); our rate can be cancelled free of charge until [date] and
> includes taxes. Compared like-for-like, our price of EUR [price] is the better offer, so we keep it.
> If your client does not need flexibility, we can quote our own non-refundable rate for the same room.

## 4. Refused - margin floor

> Thank you for flagging this. We checked the offer: the difference comes from the supplier cost for these dates,
> and we cannot go lower without selling at a loss. We would be happy to offer [hotel A] or [hotel B], similar
> location and category, at EUR [price], or the same hotel on [alternative dates].

## 5. Need more information

> Thank you for your message. To review the price, please send a screenshot taken in the last 48 hours showing the
> hotel, room type, dates, number of guests, meal plan, cancellation policy and the total price with taxes.
> We will answer within [4] hours of receiving it.

## 6. Key partner insists (escalation)

> We understand this booking matters to you. I have shared your request with our commercial manager together with
> the volume we do together; you will have an answer today. In the meantime the current rate stays on hold for
> 24 hours.

*(Internal: send the manager the partner's last-90-day volume, margin at the requested price and the like-for-like
calculation. A one-off exception is a commercial decision, not a pricing rule change.)*
