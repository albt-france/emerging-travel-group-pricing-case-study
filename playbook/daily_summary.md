# Daily summary for the commercial team

> SIMULATED data (seed 42). Not Emerging Travel Group data. The example below is generated by `src/build_playbook.py` from `data/output/sim/`.

## Format (5 blocks, readable in 2 minutes, sent before 10:00)

1. **Data status** - green / red. If red, say which check failed and that numbers are provisional.
2. **Yesterday in one line** - bookings, sales, margin rate vs expected (same weekday, last 4 weeks) and vs last year.
3. **Alerts** - only segments outside the band (|z| >= 3.5), each with a first explanation or "under review".
4. **Week trend** - last 7 days vs the 7 before, by partner type; the top 3 partner moves (sql/04).
5. **Actions and owners** - what was decided, who does it, when we check again.

Rules: numbers rounded; one idea per line; no alert without a next step; market specifics (holidays, events) from the market calendar before calling something an anomaly.

## Example - summary of Tuesday 15 September 2026, sent the next morning

**1. Data status:** green (duplicates, missing costs, time shift, FX: all checks passed).

**2. Tue 15 Sep:** 380 bookings (-9% vs expected, +11% vs last year), sales €123,037 (-13% vs expected), margin rate 7.7% (expected 7.5%).

**3. Alerts:** none at total or segment level.

**4. Week trend (sales, last 7 days vs previous 7):** Corporate -26%, White label -15%, API partner -11%, Agency -2%, B2C +6%. Largest partner move: API-002 (top API partner) at zero since 14 Sep - see incident E2.

**5. Actions:** key account manager calls API-002 today (owner: KAM, check Friday); pricing compares our rates on its top 50 hotels (owner: pricing analyst, tomorrow); Spain margin on Bedbank C still below floor - supply to confirm re-routing (owner: contracting, Thursday).
