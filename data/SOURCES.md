# Data sources

Three kinds of data, labelled everywhere in the report and the workbook:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **PUBLIC DATA** | Real, public figures, with source and collection date below |
| **REAL DATASET** | A real, open hotel-booking dataset (two hotels in Portugal, 2015-2017), used to demonstrate the analysis, **not ETG data** |
| **SIMULATED** | Generated by `src/simulate.py` (seed 42). **Not Emerging Travel Group data**: no real sales, margins, suppliers, partners or competitor prices |

## 1. Emerging Travel Group public pages (PUBLIC DATA)

Collected on **12 Nov 2026** by `src/collect_etg_site.py`: robots.txt checked for every URL (all allowed), one request
every 2.5 seconds, public marketing pages only, no login, no search results, no prices.

| Page | Used for |
|---|---|
| https://www.emergingtravel.com | headline figures (markets, accommodation options, suppliers, languages, channel managers, agents, corporate clients, travellers, direct properties, API partners, tech figures) |
| https://www.emergingtravel.com/about | employees, operating since 2010, brand descriptions |
| https://www.emergingtravel.com/brands | figures per brand (RateHawk, ZenHotels, Roundtrip) |
| https://www.emergingtravel.com/partners | 130k+ bookings weekly, 120k+ partners, 250k+ direct hotel contracts |
| https://www.emergingtravel.com/travelertype, /marketinghub | checked, not used in figures |
| https://www.ratehawk.com/ | 330+ suppliers, 220,000+ clients, 400 airlines, transfers in 165+ countries |
| https://www.zenhotels.com/about/ | checked, not used in figures |

Archived home pages (Internet Archive Wayback Machine, `src/collect_wayback.py`, one snapshot per year, collected
12 Nov 2026): 2021-07-23, 2022-07-05, 2023-07-05, 2024-07-03, 2025-07-02, 2026-06-06
(`https://web.archive.org/web/<timestamp>/https://www.emergingtravel.com/`).

Clean tables:
- `public/etg_facts.csv` - 26 facts, each with page, collection date and note;
- `public/etg_headline_history.csv` - headline figures by snapshot (2021-2026).

The raw HTML is **not** redistributed in the repository (`.gitignore`); the scripts re-create it.
Figures are marketing headlines ("190+", "110K+") and some differ between pages (190+ vs 220+ source markets,
330+ vs 350+ suppliers, 110K+ agents vs 220K+ clients, $2BN+ vs $2.6BN+ turnover "reached in 2023"
in two archived versions). The report quotes each figure with its page.

## 2. Eurostat tourism statistics (PUBLIC DATA)

Eurostat API (JSON-stat), extracted **12 Nov 2026** by `src/collect_eurostat.py`. Licence: Eurostat re-use policy,
free re-use with attribution ("Source: Eurostat").

| Dataset | Content | File |
|---|---|---|
| `tour_occ_ninraw` | Nights spent at tourist accommodation establishments by country of origin of the tourist, annual (EU27, 2019, 2023-2025) | `public/eurostat_nights_by_residence.csv` |
| `tour_dem_tnmd` | Nights spent by EU residents on trips abroad, by month of departure (10 source markets, 2023-2025) | `public/eurostat_market_seasonality.csv` |
| `tour_occ_nim` | Nights spent at tourist accommodation, monthly, residents vs non-residents, 9 destinations + EU27, Jan 2019 - Sep 2026 | `public/eurostat_monthly_nights_by_destination.csv` |

Note: Sweden's 2024 monthly series in `tour_dem_tnmd` is incomplete (December missing, unusual November), so Sweden
is left out of the seasonality chart.

## 3. ECB euro foreign exchange reference rates (PUBLIC DATA)

ECB Data Portal, series `EXR.D.<currency>.EUR.SP00.A` (units of currency per 1 EUR, daily), 1 Apr 2024 - 31 Oct 2026,
extracted **12 Nov 2026** by `src/collect_ecb_fx.py`. Currencies: USD, GBP, TRY, PLN, BRL, MXN, INR, CHF.
Weekends and holidays carry the previous business day's rate. Licence: ECB statistics may be reused with the source
cited ("Source: ECB").
Files: `raw/ecb_fx/ecb_exr_daily_2024-04-01_2026-10-31.csv`, `public/ecb_fx_daily.csv`, `simulated/fx_rates.csv`.

## 4. Hotel booking demand datasets (REAL DATASET)

Nuno Antonio, Ana de Almeida, Luis Nunes, *Hotel booking demand datasets*, Data in Brief 22 (2019) 41-49,
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2018.11.126. Licence **CC BY 4.0**.
Copy used: TidyTuesday mirror (2020-02-11),
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rfordatascience/tidytuesday/main/data/2020/2020-02-11/hotels.csv,
downloaded **12 Nov 2026** (SHA-256 `7c2ae42a7353905ea136e5c2287f17c92c5435826598bfbb8491c6f0c7b1fc06`).
Checked: 119,390 rows and 32 columns, as described in the paper (H1 resort hotel 40,060 + H2 city hotel 79,330).
File: `raw/hotel_bookings/hotels.csv`. Two hotels in Portugal, arrivals July 2015 - August 2017: used to demonstrate the
analysis, **not ETG data**.

## 5. Simulated data (SIMULATED)

`simulated/*.csv`, generated by `src/simulate.py` with seed 42. Every assumption is in `data/ASSUMPTIONS.md`.
Supplier, partner and competitor names are fictional codes ("Bedbank C", "API-002", "Competitor A").
**Competitor prices were never collected** from any website: all price-match comparisons are simulated.
